# Image-generation prompts

All assets were created with the built-in image-generation tool without input/reference images.
Transparent output was requested for every cutout and ornamental asset.

## Ganesha idol

```text

Asset type: sacred opening-screen artwork for a premium Indian wedding invitation
Primary request: create a front-facing, fully visible, seated Lord Ganesha idol suitable for a respectful royal wedding invitation.
Subject: traditional four-armed Ganesha seated calmly on a small ornate lotus pedestal, blessing hand raised, warm compassionate expression, accurate elephant anatomy and balanced iconography, elegant gold crown and restrained ruby/emerald details.
Style/medium: highly refined Indian miniature-painting-inspired digital illustration with realistic carved and painted detail, ceremonial rather than cartoonish.
Composition/framing: vertical 4:5 composition, centered and symmetrical, generous transparent margin around the entire idol, every extremity and the pedestal fully visible, nothing cropped.
Lighting/mood: warm soft golden temple light, sacred, serene, dignified.
Color palette: antique gold, ivory, muted burgundy, restrained emerald and saffron.
Constraints: genuine transparent background; no text; no halo baked into the image; no surrounding doorway; no other figures; no watermark; never crop, distort, split, tile, or tint the deity; preserve clear silhouette and fine detail at mobile size.
```
